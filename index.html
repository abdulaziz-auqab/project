Each directorate sends its files in its own format, the numbers change every week, and leadership asks where every figure came from. I wrote a pipeline that reads the incoming Excel files, merges them into one database, assigns nominees to training groups, and publishes a copy for each directorate.

Problem

Sixteen directorates, each with its own files, sheets and column names, updated weekly.

What I built

An automated path from Excel to a single offline HTML file, plus a lineage log that keeps the file, sheet, row and column behind every value.

National education forum · September 2026

From a photo of the hallto a seat for every guest

The seating system for the first edition of a national forum held with an international university partner.

The forum hosted senior guests and an international partner, so seating had no room for error on the day. I built a browser tool that detects the chairs in a photo of the hall from one color sample, draws rows and zones over them, seats 120+ leaders, then prints the plan and a name card for every guest.

Recognized with a certificate of appreciation and the forum shield.

The seating plan and name cards were ready on the morning of the event.

Qassim University · graduation project 2026

A Riyadh stone crusherwith an IRR above 60%

A feasibility study for the aggregate used in concrete, roads and buildings.

The crusher produces gravel, sand and crushed rock for Riyadh infrastructure projects. An investor needs its return and risks on one page, so I built the cash-flow model, computed NPV and IRR at a discount rate of about 5% and the payback period, and wrote a register of financial, operational and environmental risks with their mitigations.

~1.4MSAR net present value
~62%internal rate of return
~2.5years to pay back capital

Source: graduation feasibility study, Qassim University, 2026. In the same term: a research project on accounting information systems, with a survey of 90+ employees analyzed in SPSS.
